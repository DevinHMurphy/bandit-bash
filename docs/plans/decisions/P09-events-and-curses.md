# P09 decisions: Narrative events and curses

The answered questions of [the plan](../2026-11-12-events-and-curses.md), moved here on November 12, 2026; questions 7–12 were raised while building EV1. Numbers are stable.

## Questions

| # | Question | Recommendation | Answer |
|---|----------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | How event outcomes are applied | A small `EventOutcome` block set on the campaign layer, applied by a pure resolver to `RunState`, reading `CampaignGrant` and `ChangeBaseStat` data where they fit | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. Changes P01's Events contract wording, not its intent. |
| 2 | Can event damage kill a unit? | No: event damage leaves at least 1 HP | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 3 | A cursed option whose tier has no curse left that the player doesn't hold | Greyed out with the reason | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** not as recommended. Curses may repeat. An option that names a specific curse gives it even if the player already holds it. An option with a tier draws from the whole tier, held curses included. The case never arises, so nothing is greyed out. Copies stack (heirlooms.md). |
| 4 | How random event items roll | Rare weapon or tome: P08's Weapon pick rule. Trinket: Standard odds without the Rare offset. Rune: even | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 5 | Small rule readings (a)–(f) | Accept all | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. (a) Max HP loss lowers current HP only above the new max. (b) A max HP gain raises current HP. (c) Cursed Blood halves event healing. (d) Percent heals round halves up. (e) A revived unit keeps its remaining items; into a full army it works like recruiting past the max. (f) A surprise combat pays a Standard combat's full rewards. |
| 6 | Every event seen this run | The seen events become available again, as maps do | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 7 | Which campaign facts event outcomes record (EV1) | Append `GOLD_CHANGED`, `HP_CHANGED`, `UNIT_CHANGED`, `ITEM_MODIFIED` and `UNIT_REVIVED` to `CampaignFact.Kind` (append only; a lane merging second renumbers); gains keep `GAINED` | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. A rune discarded for a new one also needs a fact: `RUNE_DISCARDED` is appended with them. |
| 8 | When an option's random parts are drawn (EV1) | A roll step, before the option shows, for random items (question 4) and runes; the rare heirloom draw goes to EV2 with the curse-tier draw (no heirloom pool rule yet); the prefix re-roll draws when applied, from `EVENT_OUTCOME`. A "rare magic tome" is a Rare anima, holy or dark weapon | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 9 | The weapon modification block (EV1, for CT12) | (a) Refuse a weapon that already has a prefix, and a prefix that doesn't fit the weapon's type or staff kind. (b) A weapon prefix can't add MGT yet (no stat field, and `Loadout` ignores a prefix's effects): the test prefix uses +2 weight and +2 damage, and CT12 settles MGT. (c) Weapons and staves on living units or in the convoy can be chosen, never a fallen unit's | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 10 | Cursed Blood halving event healing, before the campaign layer reads heirloom values | The caller hands the resolver the seat's healing-received percent (100 by default) until the campaign reads its values | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 11 | Max army size, which has no per-seat value yet | The faction's max army size stands in (as for the convoy). A revive into a full army is refused until EV1F reuses RN6's replace-a-unit operation. The Company Charter's requirement checks max army size below the ceiling of 8 | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 12 | Small rule readings (a)–(f) | Accept all | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. (a) Percent damage rounds halves up and leaves at least 1 HP. (b) "Every unit" is the living army. (c) A chosen unit is living, except the shrine's; a commander's prefix can't be re-rolled. (d) A rune gained with every slot full names a slot to discard; the option is still offered, never greyed out for full slots, so runes are offered whenever available and the player is pushed to use them. (e) An option resolves all or nothing. (f) A max HP loss never takes max HP below 1. |
