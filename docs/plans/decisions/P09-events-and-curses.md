# P09 decisions: Narrative events and curses

The answered questions of [the plan](../2026-11-12-events-and-curses.md), moved here on November 12, 2026; questions 7–12 were raised while building EV1, 13–20 while building EV2, and 21–26 while building EV5. Numbers are stable.

## Questions

| # | Question | Recommendation | Answer |
|---|----------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | How event outcomes are applied | A small `EventOutcome` block set on the campaign layer, applied by a pure resolver to `RunState`, reading `CampaignGrant` and `ChangeBaseStat` data where they fit | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. Changes P01's Events contract wording, not its intent. |
| 2 | Can event damage kill a unit? | No: event damage leaves at least 1 HP | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 3 | A cursed option whose tier has no curse left that the player doesn't hold | Greyed out with the reason | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** not as recommended. Curses may repeat. An option that names a specific curse gives it even if the player already holds it. An option with a tier draws from the whole tier, held curses included. The case never arises, so nothing is greyed out. Copies stack (heirlooms.md). |
| 4 | How random event items roll | Rare weapon or tome: P08's Weapon pick rule. Trinket: Standard odds without the Rare offset. Rune: even | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 5 | Small rule readings (a)–(f) | Accept all | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. (a) Max HP loss lowers current HP only above the new max. (b) A max HP gain raises current HP. (c) Cursed Blood halves event healing. (d) Percent heals round halves up. (e) A revived unit keeps its remaining items; into a full army it works like recruiting past the max. (f) A surprise combat pays a Standard combat's full rewards. |
| 6 | Every event seen this run | The seen events become available again, as maps do | **Answered November 12, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 7 | Which campaign facts event outcomes record (EV1) | Append `GOLD_CHANGED`, `HP_CHANGED`, `UNIT_CHANGED`, `ITEM_MODIFIED` and `UNIT_REVIVED` to `CampaignFact.Kind` (append only; a lane merging second renumbers); gains keep `GAINED` | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. A rune discarded for a new one also needs a fact: `RUNE_DISCARDED` is appended with them. |
| 8 | When an option's random parts are drawn (EV1) | A roll step, before the option shows, for random items (question 4) and runes; the rare heirloom draw goes to EV2 with the curse-tier draw (no heirloom pool rule yet); the prefix re-roll draws when applied, from `EVENT_OUTCOME`. A "rare magic tome" is a Rare anima, holy or dark weapon | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 9 | The weapon modification block (EV1, for CT12) | (a) Refuse a weapon that already has a prefix, and a prefix that doesn't fit the weapon's type or staff kind. (b) A weapon prefix can't add MGT yet (no stat field, and `Loadout` ignores a prefix's effects): the test prefix uses +2 weight and +2 damage, and CT12 settles MGT. (c) Weapons and staves on living units or in the convoy can be chosen, never a fallen unit's | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 10 | Cursed Blood halving event healing, before the campaign layer reads heirloom values | The caller hands the resolver the seat's healing-received percent (100 by default) until the campaign reads its values | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 11 | Max army size, which has no per-seat value yet | The faction's max army size stands in (as for the convoy). A revive into a full army is refused until EV1F reuses RN6's replace-a-unit operation. The Company Charter's requirement checks max army size below the ceiling of 8 | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 12 | Small rule readings (a)–(f) | Accept all | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. (a) Percent damage rounds halves up and leaves at least 1 HP. (b) "Every unit" is the living army. (c) A chosen unit is living, except the shrine's; a commander's prefix can't be re-rolled. (d) A rune gained with every slot full names a slot to discard; the option is still offered, never greyed out for full slots, so runes are offered whenever available and the player is pushed to use them. (e) An option resolves all or nothing. (f) A max HP loss never takes max HP below 1. |
| 13 | Where an event's state lives and how it is driven (EV2) | `CampaignRunState.events` (an `EventRunState`), saved as the `events` section (`EventSaveSection`, CAMPAIGN, shared, version 1) and copied with the run; three `CampaignCommand`s through `CampaignService.submit_command()`, which saves: `OpenEventCommand` (the draw and the rolls; RN10's entry point), `EventChooserCommand` (the chooser stage), `EventOptionCommand` (the resolve); `EventNode` builds them from the session and `EventNode.view(session, seat)` gives EV3 an `EventView` | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 14 | The facts an event's draw and choices record (EV2) | Append `EVENT_DRAWN` (16: no seat, the event id, the node), `EVENT_CHOOSING` (17: the seat, the option index, -1 when the chooser closes) and `EVENT_CHOSEN` (18: the seat, the option, the event id, before the option's outcome facts), each with a `RunChannel` signal | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 15 | Eligibility and the draw (EV2) | (a) The shrine is eligible only when every seat has a fallen unit. (b) When no unseen event is eligible, the seen list clears and the draw uses every eligible event, as map pools refill. (c) With no event eligible, `OpenEventCommand` is rejected and the node falls back to RN10's placeholder. (d) Only inside an Unknown node, once per node; a continued run reuses the open event | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 16 | The rare heirloom draw (EV2) | `HeirloomOutcome` gains a `tier`: with no `heirloom_id`, it gives one drawn from that tier when the event is made, from the seat's `EVENT_OUTCOME` stream, evenly among the tier's heirlooms the seat doesn't own and not drawn already for the same option; co-op-only ones only in a run of more than one seat; an empty pool greys the option out. Per seat, options in order: the item and rune rolls, then the heirlooms in block order, then the tier curse | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 17 | Team choice before EV5 (EV2) | One `EventOptionCommand` resolves a team-choice option for every seat, each seat's picks and placements in it, all or nothing, and only when every seat meets its requirements; no chooser stage for team options; `votes` (seat to option index) is saved and stays empty until EV5 puts the votes and the `TEAM_CHOICE` draw in front | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 18 | Surprise combats (EV2) | Choosing one marks the event's combat pending; `CampaignRunState.battle_request()` then uses the Standard pool for the Unknown node, and the battle runs as a Standard node's (Results, the item pick, `LeaveNodeCommand` to the map). It doesn't count as a Standard combat for later steps. `run_screen.gd`'s routing of such a node to the battle screen is EV3's | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended; the routing goes to EV3. |
| 19 | Where event content comes from (EV2) | `EventPool.load_default()` lists `res://data/events/` (`ResourceLoader.list_directory`), sorted by id; an empty folder is an empty pool. EV2's test events are built in code in `tests/events/`. The section's restore checks the data's shape; a saved event id the pool lacks is reported by the view | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 20 | The chooser stage (EV2) | `EventChooserCommand(seat, option)` records that the seat is picking a unit or weapon for an option with a chooser; -1 closes it. Placements and the rune discard come with `EventOptionCommand`, so a quit while placing resumes at the chooser or the option list. After every seat has chosen, the event stays saved until the next one opens, and EV3 sends `LeaveNodeCommand` | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 21 | Where a team choice's drawn option waits (EV5) | Two steps: the last vote draws and records the option; the team's `EventOptionCommand` then applies it with each seat's picks (a drawn option may need a unit or a rune discard). The drawn option is every seat's `choosing`, which a team choice never uses otherwise (`EventRunState.drawn_option()`), so the `events` section keeps version 1 with no new key | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 22 | The facts a vote and its draw record (EV5) | Append `EVENT_VOTED` (19: the seat, the option, the event id) and `TEAM_OPTION_DRAWN` (20: no seat, the drawn option, the event id), each with a `RunChannel` signal (`event_voted`, `team_option_drawn`); a lane merging second renumbers | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 23 | The vote command (EV5) | `EventVoteCommand(seat, option)`: voting again replaces the seat's vote; no withdrawal. Refused after the draw, in an individual event, with one seat in the event (solo keeps EV2's direct `EventOptionCommand`), for an option outside the event, and for an option any seat can't meet (the reason names the seat). The vote that completes the set draws from the node's shared `TEAM_CHOICE` stream in the same command, so the last vote and the draw save together | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 24 | Applying the drawn option with more than one seat (EV5) | A team-choice `EventOptionCommand` is refused before the draw and for any option but the drawn one. Any seat may send it with every seat's picks in `team_choices`, as in EV2; P11 may restrict it to the host | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 25 | EV3's entry point for votes (EV5) | `EventNode.vote(session, seat, option, pool, catalog)` builds the vote with the `TEAM_CHOICE` stream; `EventView` gains `is_team_vote`, `votes` (a copy, seat to option), `drawn` (the drawn option, also once applied; else `NO_OPTION`), `is_vote_needed()` and `OptionView.vote_count`. `EventView.choosing` stays `NO_OPTION` in a team choice | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. |
| 26 | Small rule readings (a)–(c) (EV5) | Accept all | **Answered November 19, 2026 (user):** as recommended. (a) Votes stay saved after the draw and after the option applies, so the tally shows, until the next event opens. (b) The odds test: 3 seats voting 2 to 1 over 2000 seeds, the drawn share within 0.025 of 2/3. (c) "Combat options are always team choices" stays enforced by `EventDefinition.problems()`; the test checks that and that the toll's fight starts only through the vote. |
