# P09 decisions: Narrative events and curses

The answered questions of [the plan](../2026-11-12-events-and-curses.md), moved here on November 12, 2026; questions 7–12 were raised while building EV1, 13–20 while building EV2, 21–26 while building EV5, 27–36 while building EV3, 37–42 while building EV1F, and 43–50 while building EV4. Numbers are stable.
