# World and Theme

The setting, tone and names of Bandit Bash: who the player is, who they fight, where, and why.
It is the reference for flavor text, names, art briefs and new content, so a new enemy, heirloom or
event reads as part of one world.

**Status: the premise and tone are set; the rest is open.** The Living Labyrinth ideation
document lists "Theme/Setting/Genre" as a heading with no text, and
[game-vision.md](game-vision.md) leaves faction themes as "flavor, decided later" (Still open: "the
Brigands' real name and theme"). This document holds the premise, what the game already names, and
the questions still to answer. Answers come from the user or the ideation thread; anything not
under Premise and tone is not lore yet.

Where this document and [game-vision.md](game-vision.md) differ on a rule, the vision wins. This
document covers flavor only, never rules or values.

## Premise and tone

The user's premise (November 24, 2026):

> A great evil took over the land. It saw the rogues, mercenaries, bandits and criminals of the
> world as allies, not foes. After it succeeded in destroying the kingdoms, the bandits realized
> there was no one left to raid and pillage, so they became unlikely heroes.

**Tone:** dark fantasy with a comedic, playful undertone; not gritty. The world is in real danger,
and the people saving it are the ones nobody would have picked.

Still open within it: what the great evil is (and whether it is the darkness), and who the
title's bandits are, next to the Brigands the player fights (questions 4 and 6 below).

## What the game already names

Facts from the design docs as of November 24, 2026, with their sources. Each name can still change.

**The player's side** ([unit-roster.md](unit-roster.md), game-vision.md, Start of a run)

- **Faction:** the Mercenary, the only MVP faction.
- **Commander:** the Drengr (Old Norse for a bold warrior), with its Commander Heirloom, the Iron
  Heart.
- **Units:** Sellsword, Fighter, Lancer, Archer, Brawler, Thief, Battle Medic, Hakkapel (the
  Thirty Years' War's Finnish light cavalry), Freewing (a flier), Lance Armor, Ward, Mage, Shaman,
  Suncaster.
- The names mix plain trade names (Sellsword, Thief) with a few historical Northern European ones
  (Drengr, Hakkapel).

**The enemies** ([enemy-roster.md](enemy-roster.md))

- **Faction:** the Brigands, a placeholder name.
- **Classes:** Cutthroat, Brute, Duelist, Crossbowman, Shieldbearer, Hedge Healer, Hexer, Warcaller,
  Raider, and the Hound (a pack beast, the only non-humanoid, with Fangs).
- **Mini-bosses and the boss:** Brigand Captain, Bloodhound, Hex Matron, Iron Wall; the Powder
  Baron.
- **Planned** ([rebalance-and-features.md](rebalance-and-features.md)): the Sapper (RF-043), more
  non-humanoid creatures on a Monster weapon type (RF-052), the Slime (RF-053).
- **The Terror tag:** "monstrous or undead", enemies only ([unit-roster.md](unit-roster.md)).

**The world's systems**

- **The darkness** ([the-darkness.md](the-darkness.md)): at turn par it flows into a map from its
  openings (hallways out, stairs down, doorways into the dark), like water, in waves. It hides
  nothing and blocks nothing; it hurts units that stay in it.
- **Heirlooms** ([heirlooms.md](heirlooms.md)): run-long relics, including starting heirlooms and
  each commander's own; **curses** are their bad twins (Brittle Guard, Clouded Sight, Cursed Blood,
  Haunted, Heavy Pack, and others).
- **Runes** ([items.md](items.md#runes)): Aegis, Clarity, Haste, Mend, Recall, and others.
- **Places on a run** ([campaign-run.md](campaign-run.md)): combat maps, the Camp, the Armory, the
  Guildhall, Treasure, Unknown nodes (events), the Boss.
- **The hub** ([meta-progression.md](meta-progression.md)): the Waystation, with its war table,
  where runs start, and later the almanac and the Hall of Heroes.
- **Map objects** ([map-objects.md](map-objects.md)): doors and levers, chests, searchables
  (houses, food shelves, book shelves, weapon racks), debris, passages, powder kegs, braziers.

## How to use this document

- New content (a unit, an enemy, an heirloom, an event) checks its name and flavor text against the
  answered questions here.
- An answer is recorded in place of its row, with the user's name and date, and the open row is
  removed.
- Flavor never changes a rule. A rule a theme suggests goes to
  [rebalance-and-features.md](rebalance-and-features.md) as an entry.
